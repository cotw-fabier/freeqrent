# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Developers trying freeQRent's styles, and the maintainer tuning them. The gallery is a working sheet first: someone scrolls a grid of codes on a laptop, reads each card's command and scan verdict, and holds a phone up to the screen to check a code reads. It also gets screenshotted and shared, so it has to look good doing that.

## Product Purpose
freeQRent makes styled and animated SVG QR codes that still scan. The gallery and scan sheet show every style family with the command that reproduces it and whether it decodes. Success: a reader finds a style they like, copies its command, and trusts the verdict.

## Brand Personality
Playful, bright, confident. The name reads as "fluorescent", so the page can carry one loud highlighter color, but the codes are the show and the page never competes with them.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS dashboards: card shadows, purple-blue gradients, pill badges.
- Dark neon dev tools: black backgrounds with glowing accents.
- Beige editorial: cream paper and serif headings.
- Busy rainbow: page color fighting the codes for attention.

## Design Principles
1. The codes are the color. The page frames them and stays out of the scanner's way.
2. One loud note, used on purpose, then quiet tinted neutrals.
3. Verdicts are facts: say them in words, and let color only confirm them.
4. Practice what we preach: deterministic, light, no dependencies, works offline as one file.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Color never carries meaning alone (verdicts and warnings are written out). Reduced motion stops every animation and hides the replay button. Works in light and dark color schemes.
